Free online 3D dice and dice games

Dice Roller is a fast, free virtual dice tool with realistic 3D dice and fair random results. Use it for board games, tabletop role-playing, classroom activities, quick decisions or a bit of fun. Pick a dice style, choose a game and roll.

Five ways to play

Classic Roller

Roll 1 to 6 six-sided dice and get each number plus the total.

Tabletop D4–D20

Roll D4, D6, D8, D10, D12 or D20 with a modifier. Spot natural 20s.

Five Dice

A Yahtzee-style game: hold dice, reroll twice and score pairs, straights and full houses.

Higher or Lower

Guess if the next roll beats the last one and chase a streak.

Lucky Seven

Bet under, over or exactly 7 on two dice and build up points.

Frequently asked questions

Is this dice roller fair?

Yes. Every roll uses the browser's cryptographic random generator with unbiased selection, so every face has an equal chance.

What dice games can I play?

Classic, Tabletop D4–D20, Five Dice, Higher or Lower and Lucky Seven.

Is it free and mobile friendly?

Yes. It is free, needs no sign-up and works on phones, tablets and computers.

Can I roll with the keyboard?

Yes. Press Space or Enter to roll in any game.
