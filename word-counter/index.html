    Frequently asked questions

    How does the word counter count words?

    It splits your text into words using the browser’s language-aware segmenter, so hyphenated words, numbers, contractions and languages without spaces are handled correctly.

    How is reading time calculated?

    Reading time assumes 238 words per minute for silent reading. Speaking time assumes 150 words per minute.

    Does this tool store or upload my text?

    No. All counting happens in your browser and nothing is sent to a server. Your draft is kept in local storage on your device so it survives a refresh, and Clear removes it.

    What is the difference between characters and characters without spaces?

    Characters includes every letter, number, symbol, space and line break. Characters without spaces leaves out all whitespace.

    What are good character limits for social posts and SEO?

    Common limits are 280 for X posts, 2,200 for Instagram captions, about 60 for page titles and about 160 for meta descriptions.
