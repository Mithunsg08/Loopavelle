    What this scientific calculator can do

    This calculator covers everything from quick arithmetic to coursework in trigonometry, algebra, physics and engineering. It follows the standard order of operations, closes open brackets for you and previews the answer while you type.

    • Complex numbers: square roots of negatives, powers, logs, trig and the imaginary unit i
    • Trig: sin, cos, tan, their inverses and hyperbolic versions, in degrees, radians or gradians
    • Logarithms and exponentials: ln, log₁₀, eˣ and 10ˣ
    • Powers and roots: x², x³, xʸ, √x and ∛x
    • Factorials, combinations (nCr) and permutations (nPr)
    • Constants π and e, scientific notation with EXP, the last answer with Ans, and memory keys
    • A history panel that lists every calculation from your visit, with one-tap reuse

    How to use the scientific calculator

    Tap the keys or type on your keyboard. Press = or Enter to calculate and Escape to clear. Press 2nd to switch keys to their inverse functions, and DEG to cycle between degrees, radians and gradians before using trig functions.

    Complex numbers and the square root of a negative number

    Subtracting a larger value from a smaller one gives a negative number, and the square root of a negative number is imaginary. Instead of an error, this calculator returns the exact complex result with the imaginary unit i.

    EnterResult
    sqrt(3^2−6^2)5.19615242271i
    sqrt(-9)3i
    (1+2i)*(3−i)5 + 5i
    ln(-1)3.14159265359i
    e^(i*π)−1

    Use the Re, Im, arg and conj keys for the real part, imaginary part, argument and conjugate. abs(z) gives the modulus.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the square root of a negative number?

    It is an imaginary number. This calculator returns it with the imaginary unit i, so sqrt(-9) gives 3i and sqrt(3^2−6^2) gives 5.19615242271i.

    How do I switch between degrees and radians?

    Press the DEG key to cycle through degrees (DEG), radians (RAD) and gradians (GRAD). The active mode is shown on the display.

    How do I use inverse trig functions like sin⁻¹?

    Press 2nd. The sin, cos, tan, sinh, cosh and tanh keys change to their inverses, and ln, log, √x and x² change to eˣ, 10ˣ, ∛x and x³.

    Does the calculator keep my history?

    Every calculation from the current visit is listed in the history panel. Nothing is saved or uploaded, and the history starts empty again when you refresh the page.

    How do I calculate combinations and permutations?

    Press nCr or nPr, enter n, a comma, then r. For example nCr(10,3) returns 120 and nPr(10,3) returns 720.

    Can I type with my keyboard?

    Yes. Type numbers and operators directly, press Enter to calculate and Escape to clear. Function names such as sin(, log( and sqrt( can be typed too.
