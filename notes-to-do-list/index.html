    A simple notes app and to do list with reminders, saved on your phone

    Notes and To Do keeps your notes and your daily task list in one place. Write colourful notes, pin the ones you need first and find any note in seconds with search. Add tasks with due dates, choose to be reminded on the day, 1 day before or 2 days before, and watch your progress ring fill as you tick things off.

    What you can do

    • Write notes with a title, pin them to the top and pick a colour.
    • Search every note as you type.
    • Plan tasks with due dates and a high priority flag.
    • Get reminders such as "Today is the last day to do" or "Only 2 days left to do".
    • Filter your to do list by All, Active or Done.
    • Use it in light or dark mode on a phone, tablet or computer.

    How your data is stored

    Everything you write is saved in your browser's storage on this device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Your notes and tasks are still here the next time you open the page. If you clear your browsing data or remove the browser or app that holds it, they are deleted too, so copy out anything you cannot lose.

    Questions

    Is Notes and To Do free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no account and no ads.

    How do reminders work?

    Choose a due date, then pick when to be reminded. Reminders appear at the top of the app, and as a phone alert when the page is open and you have allowed alerts.

    Will I get a reminder if the app is closed?

    Reminders appear when you open Notes and To Do and while it stays open. A closed page cannot send alerts, so open it to see what is due.

    Where are my notes and tasks saved?

    In your browser's storage on the device you are using. Nothing is sent to a server.

    What happens if I clear browser data or delete the app?

    Your notes and tasks are removed with it. If you leave that data alone, they stay exactly as you left them.

    Will my notes appear on my other devices?

    No. Items stay on the device and browser where you wrote them, so open the page the same way each time.
