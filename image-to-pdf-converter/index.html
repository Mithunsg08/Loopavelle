Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Universal Converter works inside your browser, so your files never leave your device.

How do I convert MP4 to MP3?

Drop the MP4, choose MP3 and wait for 100%. The audio downloads automatically.

How do I convert JPG to PNG or PNG to JPG?

Upload the image, pick PNG, JPG or WebP, and the converted image downloads automatically.

Can I convert PDF to Word, Excel or PowerPoint?

PDF to Word (text) works. PDF to Excel and PowerPoint need server-side layout analysis, so they are not offered, to avoid broken results.

Why is video conversion slower?

Video is re-encoded on your own device. Large files take longer, and the engine (about 30 MB) downloads once on first use.
