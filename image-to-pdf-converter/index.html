How it works

1. Upload your file. 2. We detect the type and show the formats you can convert to. 3. Pick one, watch the progress from 1% to 100%, and the file downloads automatically. Conversion happens locally with WebAssembly and Canvas, so it is fast and private. Large video files depend on your device's memory.

FAQ

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Every conversion runs inside your browser, so your files never leave your device.

How do I convert MP4 to MP3?

Drop your MP4, choose MP3, and the audio is extracted and downloaded automatically.

Can I convert PDF to Word, Excel or PowerPoint?

Yes. PDF to Word and Excel extract the text and rows (scanned PDFs without text need OCR). PDF to PowerPoint turns each page into a slide.
