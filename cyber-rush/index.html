Cyber Rush: a free 3D cyberpunk endless runner

Cyber Rush is a fast, free 3D runner you play right in your browser — no download, no account. You pilot a neon cybercraft down a five-lane grid above a glowing city, dodging rock walls, laser gates and low barriers while sudden ambushes tear in from the dark: meteors, rogue jets and drone swarms. Each level is longer, faster and meaner than the last.

Everything is built for skill. Chain close calls into combo multipliers, jump barriers, slide under laser gates, burn boost energy for double credits and finish contracts for big payouts. Credits are never handed out — they are earned run by run, then spent in the 4D holographic garage.

Hazard guide

☄ Meteor strikes

A red target ring appears on the track and a fireball plunges onto it about a second later. Leave the lane before impact — the crater stays as a rock.

✈ Rogue jets

A lane glows red, then a jet dives down it at extreme speed. Shift lanes the moment the warning lights up.

◉ Drone swarms

Drones sweep sideways across the grid and arrive in the lane they were aimed at. Time a lane change or jump over them.

⛰ Rock walls

Solid cyber-rock blocks lanes. Higher levels block up to four lanes at once, so read the gaps early.

▬ Low barriers

Jump them. Coin arcs float above barriers and reward clean jumps with bonus credits.

⫿ Laser gates

Slide under the glowing beam. A mid-air S/↓ slams you down into a slide.

Earning credits the hard way

Credits come from distance, coins, close calls, perfect jumps and slides, and level-clear bonuses. Your combo multiplier climbs to ×8 while you keep scoring and drops if you stall or take a hit. A flawless level pays a no-damage bonus. Crash and you keep only half of that run's credits, so surviving matters.

Three rotating contracts ask for things like close calls, coins, total distance or combo level. Finish one and a bigger one takes its place.

The 4D garage: ships, paint and upgrades

The garage renders a real four-dimensional hypercube (a tesseract) rotating through the fourth dimension around your ship, so you can inspect every craft in a living hologram. Drag to spin it. Six ships are unlockable by reaching level milestones and paying credits, from the starter Apex Runner to the Eclipse Sovereign.

Six upgrades — Handling, Overdrive Core, Magnet Field, Armor Plating, Chrono Warp and Credit Matrix — each have five levels and rising prices. Paint jobs change your ship's colour everywhere.

Pro tips for higher levels

Stay in the center lanes for the most escape routes. Save boost for open stretches and coin lines. Watch the top banner: it names the incoming hazard before it appears. Collect blue shield orbs, pink magnets and purple chrono orbs. And when a gate and a rock wall arrive together, jump or slide first and change lanes second.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Cyber Rush?

Use A/D or the arrow keys to switch lanes, W or Up to jump, S or Down to slide, and Space or Shift to boost. On phones, swipe left, right, up or down and use the on-screen boost button.

How do I earn credits in Cyber Rush?

Earn credits from distance, coins, close calls, jump and slide clears, combo multipliers, contracts and level-clear bonuses. A no-damage level clear pays a bonus, while a crash only salvages half of that run's credits.

What are the sudden hazards?

Meteors fall onto a marked lane, rogue jets dive down a glowing lane at high speed, and drone swarms sweep sideways across the track. Each gives a brief warning, so watch the alert banner and the red lane markers.

What is the 4D garage?

The garage is a holographic hangar with a live rotating tesseract, a four-dimensional hypercube, projected around your ship. Drag to rotate your craft, buy new ships, repaint them and upgrade six performance systems.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Credits, levels, ships, upgrades and contracts are saved in your browser with local storage. Nothing is sent to a server.
