Global Postal Code Formats

United States (ZIP Code)

5 numeric digits (e.g., 90210) or ZIP+4 extended format (90210-1234).

United Kingdom (Postcode)

Alphanumeric, 5-7 characters with outward/inward codes (e.g., SW1A 1AA).

India (PIN Code)

6 numeric digits representing postal zone, sub-zone, and post office (e.g., 110001).

Japan (Postal Code)

7 digits with hyphen mark symbol 〒 (e.g., 100-0001).

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do some locations show "N/A"?

Certain nations—such as the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Hong Kong, and Ireland—do not utilize standard numeric postal codes. Instead, they rely on building numbers, Makani smart identifiers, or zone systems. Earth's Zipcode accurately identifies these countries and explains their localized system.

Is all location data presented in English?

Yes! All geocoding responses pass through an English normalization pipeline to translate Cyrillic, CJK, Arabic, and Devanagari names into standardized Latin English characters.
