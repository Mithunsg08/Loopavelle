Everything you need to make something worth posting

Emulsion pairs a layer-based image editor with a multi-track video timeline. It opens in one click, runs offline once loaded, and works in full screen when you want the room.

Layer-based image editor

Text, shapes, photos and freehand strokes as separate layers. Reorder, lock, hide, align, group-select and nudge with the arrow keys.

Starter templates

Six layouts for thumbnails, posters, quotes, sale banners and announcements. Swap the photo, change the words, export.

Photo adjustments

Brightness, contrast, saturation, blur and hue sliders, plus one-click looks. Mask any photo to a circle or rounded frame.

Video timeline

Import clips and stills, trim in and out points, split at the playhead, change speed, set volume and fade through black.

Captions and titles

Timed text overlays with font, colour, size and entry animation, plus generated title cards with moving gradients.

Export where you need it

PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG or project JSON for images. Video exports as MP4 or WebM, depending on what your browser records.

Full screen workspace

One button expands the editor to the whole display. Press F to toggle it, Esc to leave.

Private by design

Photos and footage are processed on your device. There is no upload step and nothing to sign in to.

Quick guides

Make a YouTube thumbnail

  1. Open Templates and pick the thumbnail layout.
  2. Drop your photo on the stage, then set its size to cover the frame.
  3. Double-click the headline and type three to five words.
  4. Raise contrast on the photo from the Properties panel.
  5. Choose PNG at 1x and press Export.

Create an Instagram post

  1. Open Size and choose Instagram post, 1080 x 1080.
  2. Pick the quote or sale template.
  3. Change the colours in Properties.
  4. Export as JPG to keep the file small.

Edit a short video

  1. Switch to Video and import your clip.
  2. Select it and drag the trim sliders to set the in and out points.
  3. Press S at the playhead to split, then delete what you do not need.
  4. Add a text overlay and drag it on the Text track.
  5. Press Export and keep the tab open while it records.

Questions

Is Emulsion really free?

Yes. There is no watermark, no sign-in and no paid tier. Every tool on this page is available to everyone.

Where are my files stored?

In your browser while the page is open. Photos and video are read on your device and never uploaded. Export a project JSON if you want to reopen a design later.

Which browsers work best?

Current Chrome, Edge and Firefox support everything, including video export. Safari handles image editing and playback, but colour looks and video recording may be limited.

Why does video export take as long as the video?

The browser records the timeline as it plays, which keeps audio and picture in sync without any server. Keep the tab visible while it renders.

Can I use it on a phone?

The layout stacks into one column on small screens, and touch works for selecting, dragging and scrubbing. Large video edits are more comfortable on a laptop.

Does it have AI tools such as background removal?

Not yet. Emulsion focuses on layout, type, colour and cutting, and does those without sending your media anywhere.
