Ship's Log: Questions

How does the online compass work?

It reads your device's orientation sensor in the browser to show your heading. On devices without a sensor you can steer the dial by dragging it.

Is my location sent to a server?

No. Everything runs client-side in your browser. Your location and heading never leave your device.

How do I find the bearing to a destination?

Enter the destination latitude and longitude, press Set Course, and the compass shows the bearing, the distance and which way to turn.

Why is my compass reading off?

Phone compasses can be disturbed by magnets and metal. Move away from them and wave your device in a figure eight to recalibrate. Readings are magnetic north as reported by your device.
