Frequently asked questions

How do I compare time zones for a meeting?

Add the cities you need, then drag the time-travel slider. Every city updates instantly so you can find an hour that works for everyone.

How accurate is the online stopwatch?

The stopwatch displays hundredths of a second and keeps running accurately even if the tab is in the background, because it measures against the system clock.

Does the world clock handle daylight saving time?

Yes. Chronofern uses your browser's built-in IANA time zone database, so daylight saving changes are applied automatically for every city.

Is my data stored on a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your cities and settings are saved only in your own device's local storage.

Can I export my lap times?

Yes. Copy laps to the clipboard or download them as a CSV file with one click.
