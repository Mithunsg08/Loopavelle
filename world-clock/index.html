Frequently asked questions

What is the current time in the USA?

World Clock shows New York (Eastern Time) by default, with live Central, Mountain, Pacific, Alaska and Hawaii clocks right below. Daylight saving time is handled automatically.

Can I see the time in any country?

Yes. Search any country or city, add it to your list and it is saved in your browser.

How do I plan a meeting across time zones?

Drag the time-travel slider to move every clock forward or backward and see the same moment in all your cities.

How do I export stopwatch laps?

Press Lap while the stopwatch runs, then use Copy to copy the laps or CSV to download them as a spreadsheet file.
