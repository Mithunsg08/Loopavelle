The most beautiful way to play your music online

Music Player is a free browser music player with a spinning 3D vinyl, a live audio visualizer and full-screen mode. Play songs from your device, sign in with Spotify, or paste a YouTube, SoundCloud, Apple Music or Deezer link. Nothing to install.

Play local audio

Drag in MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A, OGG or Opus files, or a whole folder.

One-click Spotify

Sign in, search, open playlists and liked songs (Premium for full playback).

More services

YouTube, SoundCloud, Apple Music, Deezer and direct audio links.

3D vinyl & visualizer

Choose ring, wave or mirror bars. Colors adapt to every cover.

Full-screen mode

Private

Frequently asked questions

Is Music Player free?

Yes, with no sign-up.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Local files are played by your browser and never uploaded.

Can I play Spotify?

Yes. Click Continue with Spotify. Full songs need Premium; free accounts can paste Spotify links.

Keyboard shortcuts?

Space play/pause · ←→ seek · ↑↓ volume · N next · P previous · S shuffle · R repeat · M mute · V visualizer · F full screen · Z focus.
