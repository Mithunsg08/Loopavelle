Frequently asked questions

Quick answers.

What links work?

Direct file links (.mp4, .webm, .mp3, .wav, .jpg, .png, .webp, .gif, .pdf, .zip and more) from hosts that allow browser downloads.

Is it private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. Links and history never leave your device.

Can I download from YouTube, Instagram or TikTok?

No. Those are web pages, not direct files, and their terms restrict downloading. Use their official save options or content you have permission to keep.

Why did a download fail?

Usually the host blocks cross-site requests (CORS). Use the Open link button to save it with your browser instead.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up.
