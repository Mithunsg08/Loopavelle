Frequently asked questions

Can I download private media?

No. If a link is private we say so and stop. We focus on privacy, so we never try to bypass it. Ask the owner to make it public, or try another public link.

Do you store my links or files?

No. Links are used only to find the public file, and your result list lives in your browser tab until you refresh.

Which formats are supported?

Video (MP4, WebM), audio (MP3, M4A) and images (JPG, PNG, WebP), depending on what the public source offers.

Is it legal to download media?

Only download content you own, that is in the public domain, or that you have permission to save. You are responsible for respecting copyright and each site's terms.
