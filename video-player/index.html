              Free online video player for local files, live TV and streaming

              Video Player is a free, private media player that runs in your browser. Import every video on your device with one tap, watch live TV from more than a hundred countries, search YouTube, Dailymotion and the Internet Archive, download captions by movie name and keep a watch history you can switch off.

              It works on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS. Your library, history, settings, saved captions and the Live TV channel list stay on your device, so they open without internet. Only the live broadcasts and streaming sites need a connection to play.

              How to use Video Player

              1. Import. Press Import all and allow your browser to read a folder. Your videos are listed instantly.
              2. Play. Click any video, channel or search result. Use the settings panel for speed, brightness and themes.
              3. Add captions. Press CC, search the movie name online or load a local file. It is saved for next time.
              4. Go offline. Open Live TV once online so the channel list is saved, then browse it anywhere.

              Supported formats and sources

              Local videoMP4 (H.264/AAC), WebM, OGV, MOV, M4V. MKV and other codecs depend on your browser.
              Local audioMP3, M4A, AAC, FLAC, WAV, Ogg and Opus.
              CaptionsSRT and WebVTT, local or downloaded online.
              Live TVHLS (.m3u8) channels from public community playlists.
              StreamingYouTube, Dailymotion, Internet Archive, Twitch, Vimeo and direct links.

              Watch live TV and local videos without internet

              Video Player saves the full Live TV channel list on your device and caches the app itself. That means you can search channels by name, country and category even when you are offline, and play every video in your imported folders. A live broadcast is delivered by the TV station over the internet, so the picture itself needs a connection; the player tells you clearly when it is offline.

              People use it as an offline video player for trips and flights, an MKV and MP4 library viewer, an IPTV and HLS player, a YouTube and Dailymotion search player, a subtitle downloader and a private alternative to desktop media players.

              Frequently asked questions

              What is Video Player?

              Video Player is a free media player that runs in your web browser. It plays videos stored on your device, streams live TV channels from around the world, searches YouTube, Dailymotion and the Internet Archive, finds captions online and keeps an optional watch history.

              Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

              No. Import all only asks your browser for permission to read a folder. Files play directly from your device and are never uploaded.

              How do I import all videos from a folder?

              Press the large Import all button and allow access when your browser asks. Every video in the folder and its sub-folders appears in a searchable library. Chrome and Edge remember the folder so you can reconnect in one click.

              Does Video Player work without internet?

              Yes. After the first visit the page, your imported library, history, settings, saved captions and the Live TV channel list work offline. Online streams such as YouTube need a connection.

              Can I watch live TV offline?

              The Live TV channel list is saved on your device so you can browse and search channels offline. The live broadcast itself comes from the TV station over the internet, so playing a channel needs a connection.

              How do I add captions or subtitles?

              Open the CC button. Online is the default: type the movie name, choose a language and pick a caption file. It is downloaded, stored on your device and played in the video. You can also load a local SRT or VTT file or reuse a saved caption.

              Where are downloaded captions stored?

              Captions are stored in your browser on your device. The next time you open the same video the saved caption loads automatically. You can delete saved captions in Settings.

              How do I turn off watch history?

              Use the Save history switch in the History tab or in Settings. When it is off nothing new is recorded, and you can clear existing history at any time.

              Which video formats are supported?

              MP4 (H.264/AAC), WebM, OGV, MOV, M4V, MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV and Ogg play in major browsers. MKV depends on your browser. Live streams use HLS (.m3u8).

              Can I change the colour theme?

              Yes. Choose Midnight, Light, Aurora, Sunset, Ocean or Rose in Settings. Midnight, a black and white iPhone-style dark mode, is the default.

              Video Player is a free, private media player and does not host any channels or videos.
