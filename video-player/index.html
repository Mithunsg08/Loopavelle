        Video Player: a free online video player for local files, live TV and streaming

        Video Player is a free, beautiful media player that runs entirely in your web browser. Open videos from your device, watch live TV channels from more than a hundred countries, search YouTube, Dailymotion and the Internet Archive, or jump straight into a Twitch stream — all from one screen, with no installation, no account and no uploads. Your files and settings never leave your device.

        Unlike a traditional desktop app, Video Player works on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS in any modern browser. Bookmark it and you have a VLC-style player with a cinematic design, picture-in-picture, subtitles, playback speed control and one-tap fullscreen wherever you go.

        Features of a modern browser video player

        Local video player & library

        Drag and drop MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV, MP3, FLAC and more. Files are kept in your browser's own storage so you can resume where you stopped and export the whole library any time.

        Live TV from around the world

        Browse thousands of free IPTV channels by country and category, search by channel name, save favourites and import your own M3U playlist.

        Search, then stream

        Choose a platform, search any topic, see the results and play instantly. YouTube, Dailymotion, Internet Archive, Twitch and Kick are built in, and direct links and HLS/DASH streams work too.

        3D, 4D, 5D and 6D modes

        Go from a flat screen to depth-tilt 3D, time-echo 4D, sound-reactive 5D and the all-in-one 6D cosmos mode — an immersive way to watch movies, music videos and live streams.

        Fullscreen & cinema controls

        One-tap fullscreen with auto-hiding controls, landscape lock on phones, theater mode, picture-in-picture, a frame that adapts to any aspect ratio and keyboard shortcuts for everything.

        Private by design

        Everything is processed in your browser. There are no accounts and no server uploads, and you can clear your library with a single click.

        How to use Video Player in 3 steps

        1. Choose your source. Pick Local, Live TV or Stream from the side panel.
        2. Search or select. Drop video files, search channels by name, country or category, or choose a platform and search a topic.
        3. Click and watch. The video starts instantly. Press F for fullscreen and V to switch between 2D, 3D, 4D, 5D and 6D.

        Frequently asked questions

        What is Video Player?

        Video Player is a free media player that runs in your web browser. It plays local video and audio files, streams live TV channels from around the world, searches YouTube, Dailymotion and the Internet Archive, and opens Twitch and direct links.

        Is Video Player free and private?

        Yes. It is free, needs no account and processes everything in your browser. Local files are never uploaded; your library and settings are stored only on your device.

        Which video formats can it play?

        MP4 (H.264/AAC), WebM, OGV, MOV, M4V, MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV and Ogg play in all major browsers. MKV and some codecs depend on what your browser supports. Live streams use HLS (.m3u8) and DASH (.mpd).

        How do I watch live TV channels?

        Open the Live TV tab, type a channel name in the search box or choose a country and category, then click a channel to play it. You can favourite channels and import your own M3U playlist.

        How do I search and play YouTube videos?

        Open the Stream tab, pick YouTube, type a topic and press Search. Click any result to play it. You can also paste a YouTube link, and Dailymotion, Internet Archive, Twitch and Kick work the same way.

        What do the 3D, 4D, 5D and 6D modes do?

        3D tilts the screen in perspective and follows your cursor. 4D adds time-echo trails and ripples. 5D reacts to sound with glowing bars and particles. 6D combines all effects with a parallax cosmos background.

        Where are my local videos stored and can I export them?

        Videos you add are saved in your browser's own storage on your device. Use Export all to write them to a folder or download them, or remove them any time with Clear.

        Why does a channel or video not play?

        Some live streams go offline or block playback in browsers, and some sites do not allow embedding. Try another channel or result, or open a direct video link.
