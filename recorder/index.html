Recorder: the free online voice, video and screen recorder

Recorder is a fast, modern online recorder that works right in your browser. Capture your voice, record video with your front or back camera, or record your computer screen – then play it back, rename it, share it or download it. There is nothing to install, no account to create and no watermark. Because everything is processed on your device, your recordings stay private.

How to record voice or video online

  1. Choose a mode and tap record. Pick Voice, Video or Screen, allow microphone or camera access, and press the big record button.
  2. Pause, mark or go fullscreen. Use the pause button any time, add markers with the flag, or open fullscreen for a clean workspace.
  3. Stop and keep it. Tap stop and the recording is saved on your device. Play it, rename it, share it or download the file.

Why use a browser-based recorder?

Traditional recording apps need to be downloaded, updated and given broad permissions. A web recorder opens instantly on phones, tablets, Chromebooks and computers. Recorder uses the browser's built-in MediaRecorder technology, so audio and video are encoded locally and saved straight to private on-device storage. That means faster start-up, less storage used by apps and complete control over your files.

Tips for better recordings

  • Hold the phone 15–20 cm from your mouth and speak across it, not directly into it, to reduce pops.
  • Record in a quiet, soft room – curtains, carpets and sofas reduce echo.
  • Use a headset or external microphone and pick it under Settings → Microphone.
  • Use the countdown so you have time to get ready before recording begins.
  • Turn on Do Not Disturb so calls and notifications do not interrupt your take.
  • For video, face a window or soft light and keep the camera at eye level.
  • Add markers for key moments, then jump to them when reviewing.

Recording formats explained

Your browser chooses the best format it supports. WebM (Opus audio, VP8/VP9 video) is common on Chrome, Edge and Firefox. MP4 (AAC audio, H.264 video) is used by Safari. OGG (Opus) may appear on some Firefox builds. All are widely playable, and most editors and messaging apps accept them. If you need another format, convert the downloaded file with any free converter.

Privacy and security

Recorder does not send audio, video or screen content to any server. Files are stored in your browser's IndexedDB on your device and are visible only to you. Microphone and camera access is requested by your browser and is only active while you record. When you stop, all capture devices are released immediately. To remove recordings permanently, delete them in the app or clear the site's data in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Is this online recorder really free?

Yes. Recorder is completely free with no sign-up, no watermark and no time limit other than your device storage.

Where are my recordings stored?

In your browser's private storage on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Can I pause a recording and continue later?

Yes. Pause and resume work in voice, video and screen modes. The timer stops while paused and the final file is one continuous recording.

Does the recorder work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It works in modern mobile browsers such as Safari, Chrome and Firefox. Screen recording is only available on desktop browsers.

How do I use fullscreen mode?

Tap the fullscreen button next to the mode switch. Only the recorder and your recordings are shown, without the rest of the page.

What are markers?

Markers are timestamp flags you add while recording by tapping the flag button. Tap a marker later to jump straight to that moment.

What format are the recordings?

The format depends on your browser: usually WebM or MP4 for video and WebM, MP4 or OGG for audio. These play on almost every device.

How do I keep a recording permanently?

Tap the download button on a recording to save the file to your device. Clearing browser data removes recordings that were not downloaded.

Can I choose which microphone to use?

Yes. Open Settings and select any connected microphone, including headsets and USB mics.

Why is my microphone not working?

Allow microphone access in the browser prompt, check that no other app is using the mic, and make sure the page is opened over HTTPS.
