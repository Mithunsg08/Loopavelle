Frequently asked questions

Is this calculator really free and private?

Yes. Every calculation runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server, stored or tracked.

How do I calculate the fuel cost of a trip?

Divide the distance by your vehicle's mileage to get the fuel needed, then multiply by the fuel price. The fuel cost calculator does this for km/L, L/100km and MPG, handles round trips and splits the cost between passengers.

How do I calculate a tip and split the bill?

Multiply the bill by the tip percentage, add it to the bill, then divide by the number of people. The tip calculator also offers a round-up option.

How is EMI calculated?

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly instalments.

How do I add or remove GST or VAT from a price?

To add tax, multiply the net price by (1 + rate). To remove tax from a gross price, divide by (1 + rate). The GST calculator does both and shows the CGST/SGST split.
